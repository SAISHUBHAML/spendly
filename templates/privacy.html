Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information Spendly collects when you use the app, how we use it, and the choices you have. We collect only what we need to help you track your expenses.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us directly when you create an account and use the service.

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and password.
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.
  • Basic technical information needed to keep you signed in, such as session cookies.

Spendly does not connect to your bank accounts or cards, and we do not collect payment card numbers.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information only to provide and improve the service.

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To store your expenses and show you summaries and breakdowns of your spending.
  • To respond to you when you contact us.
  • To keep the service reliable and protect it against misuse.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in Spendly's application database. Passwords are stored in hashed form rather than as plain text, and access to your expense records is limited to your own account.

We keep your data for as long as your account is open. You may delete individual expenses at any time, and you may ask us to delete your account and the data associated with it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers when a page loads. That request is governed by Google's own privacy policy.

Beyond this, we do not share your personal data with third parties unless we are required to do so by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or about how your data is handled, write to us at privacy@spendly.example.
